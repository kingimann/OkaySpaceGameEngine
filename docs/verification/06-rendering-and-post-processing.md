- [ ] **V-RN-001** Look presets (sky, fog, post-processing in one go)
  - **Map:** 2, 26. **Where:** editor, Lighting panel
  - **Steps:**
    1. Open **View > Panels > Lighting** and the **Post-processing** section.
    2. Open the **Look Preset** combo and pick a preset (for example Sunset or Night).
    3. Press **Reset** next to it.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** the Scene and Game views change sky, fog and post-processing together; Reset returns to plain colour sky, no fog, no post. The line under the preset says "GPU post chain: on" or "off".
  - **Known partial:** the Scene view draws through SDL's 2D renderer, so what the GPU chain looks like may differ from a built game (map 38).

- [ ] **V-RN-002** Bloom, tonemap, exposure, vignette
  - **Map:** 2, 26. **Where:** editor, Lighting panel
  - **Steps:**
    1. In **Post-processing** tick **Bloom** and drag **Exposure**.
    2. Change **Tonemap** in the combo, and raise **Vignette**.
    3. Untick them again.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** bright areas glow with Bloom; the picture darkens or lightens with Exposure; corners darken with Vignette.
  - **Known partial:** none known.
