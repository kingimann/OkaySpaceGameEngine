City council approves a new park on the old rail yard

By Ana Torres · 26 November 2026 · 4 min read

After two years of public meetings, the city council voted seven to two on Tuesday to turn the disused rail yard on the east bank into a public park with gardens, a playground and a path along the water.

The plan keeps the old signal box as a café and reuses the rails as edging for the flower beds. Work starts in spring and the first part should open the following summer.

"People asked for green space close to home, and this is the biggest piece of land we had left," the mayor said.

Two councillors voted against, saying the money should go to road repairs first. The budget for the first phase is set at four million.
