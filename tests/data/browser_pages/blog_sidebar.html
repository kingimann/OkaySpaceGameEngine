Sharpening chisels without a jig

Posted on 3 November · 12 comments

For years I used a honing guide for everything. Last winter I tried freehand sharpening on the narrow chisels, and it turned out to be faster and good enough for most work.

The trick is to feel the bevel sit flat on the stone, then lift the handle a hair. A secondary bevel is quicker to refresh than the whole face.

1000 grit  - 20 strokes
4000 grit  - 15 strokes
8000 grit  - 10 strokes, then strop
