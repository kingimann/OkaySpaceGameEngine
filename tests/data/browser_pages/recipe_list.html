Lemon drizzle cake

Serves 8Prep 20 minBake 45 min

Ingredients

  • 225 g butter
  • 225 g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 225 g flour
  • 2 lemons
  • 85 g icing sugar

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a loaf tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then add the eggs one at a time.
  3. Fold in the flour and the zest of one lemon.
  4. Bake for 45 minutes; pour the lemon juice and icing sugar over while warm.

Tip: prick the cake all over with a skewer so the drizzle soaks in.

Keeps
Three days in a tin.
Freezes
Yes, without the drizzle.
