Lighthouse

Lighthouse
Height42 m2 base
First lit1874[1]
RangeH2O spray: 18 km

A lighthouse is a tower built to send light out to sea[1], so ships can find the coast at night. The light turns about once every 101 seconds, and older towers burned oil[2] before electric lamps came. Water vapour (H2O) and salt wear the glass, so keepers clean it every day.

The area of the lens is about 3 m2; its focal length is written f0 in the old logs[3].
