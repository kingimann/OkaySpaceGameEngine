# Verify: Characters and animation

Models, player controllers, cameras, stances, animation, animator, IK.

Generated by `scripts/gen_verify_checklist.py` from the feature map and `docs/verification/steps/CH.md`; do not edit by hand. Read the [guide](README.md) first (legend and how to report).

Areas: 3, 4, 5, 30. Rows ids start `V-CH-`. `V-CH-S<area>` rows are smoke tests built from the map; `V-CH-NNN` rows have written steps.

- [ ] **V-CH-001** First person player
  - **Map:** 4.1, 4.2. **Where:** editor
  - **Steps:**
    1. Choose **File > New Project...** and the **First and Third Person** template (or **GameObject > Player > Player Controller**), then press **V** once to go to the eyes (or set View to First Person in the Player Controller panel).
    2. Press Play (Ctrl+P). Walk with W A S D and look with the mouse.
    3. Press Ctrl to crouch and Z to go prone (crouch toggles with a tap by default; prone key is Z).
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** you walk and look in first person; the camera lowers when you crouch and lowers more when prone; running is off while crouched or prone.
  - **Known partial:** map 4.2: prone on first and third person is listed as "verify"; hands-on-ledge IK is not wired.
  - **More:** [../../try_it.md](../../try_it.md)

- [ ] **V-CH-002** Third person player
  - **Map:** 4.1, 4.2. **Where:** editor
  - **Steps:**
    1. Choose the **First and Third Person** template (or **GameObject > Player > Third Person**).
    2. Press Play. Move with W A S D, look with the mouse, jump with Space.
    3. Press Z for prone and Ctrl for crouch.

  - **Known partial:** none known beyond map 4.2.

- [ ] **V-CH-003** Reduce Motion stops the head bob
  - **Map:** 4.1, 8, 12. **Where:** editor, in a First Person scene
  - **Steps:**
    1. In a First Person scene press Play, walk, and watch the camera bob. Press Stop.
    2. Choose **GameObject > UI > Prebuilt Screens > Settings** to add a Settings panel.
    3. Press Play, click **Reduce motion** on the panel, click **Done**, and walk again.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** with Reduce motion on the bob and sway are gone and there is no dip on landing; off, they come back.
  - **Known partial:** the editor itself has no accessibility window; the switch lives on the game's Settings screen.
  - **More:** [../../try_it.md](../../try_it.md)

- [ ] **V-CH-004** Turn interpolation
  - **Map:** 4.1, 6. **Where:** editor
  - **Steps:**
    1. In a **Third Person** scene choose **Edit > Project Settings...**, open the Physics tab, set **Steps per second** to 10 and tick **Smooth the motion**.
    2. Press Play and walk in a tight circle.
    3. Untick **Smooth the motion** and compare.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** with smoothing the motion is even; without it, at 10 steps a second, it visibly steps.
  - **Known partial:** the "before the fix" look needs an older release to compare.
  - **More:** [../../try_it.md](../../try_it.md)

- [ ] **V-CH-007** Foot IK on a player
  - **Map:** 5, 4. **Where:** editor, GameObject menu
