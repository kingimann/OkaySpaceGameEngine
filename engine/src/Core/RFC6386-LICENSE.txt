The constant tables in ImageDecodeWebpTables.hpp (VP8 key-frame sub-block mode probabilities, default and update
coefficient probabilities, DC and AC quantiser steps) are data taken from RFC 6386, "VP8 Data Format and Decoding
Guide" (J. Bankoski, J. Koleszar, L. Quillio, J. Salonen, P. Wilkins, Y. Xu, November 2011,
https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc6386), sections 11.5, 13.4, 13.5 and 14.1. The decoder that uses them
(ImageDecodeWebp.cpp) is our own code, written from the RFC's text.

The RFC is subject to BCP 78 and the IETF Trust's Legal Provisions Relating to IETF Documents; tables of values
extracted from it are used as Code Components under the Simplified BSD License that follows (wording of the IETF
Trust Legal Provisions, the licence the Brotli dictionary notice in this folder also carries; not re-fetched for this
file). The same tables are also printed in the RFC's attached reference decoder (section 20), whose licence (section
20.26) follows the first one.
