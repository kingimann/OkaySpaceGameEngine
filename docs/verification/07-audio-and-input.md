# Verify: Audio and input

Audio sources, mixer, spatial sound, and keyboard, mouse, gamepad and touch input.

Generated by `scripts/gen_verify_checklist.py` from the feature map and `docs/verification/steps/AU.md`; do not edit by hand. Read the [guide](README.md) first (legend and how to report).

Areas: 7, 8. Rows ids start `V-AU-`. `V-AU-S<area>` rows are smoke tests built from the map; `V-AU-NNN` rows have written steps.

- [ ] **V-AU-001** Play a sound from an Audio Source
  - **Map:** 7. **Where:** editor, Inspector
  - **Steps:**
    1. Select an object and press **Add Component**, choose **Audio Source**.
    2. In its section press **Beep** (a generated tone) or choose an audio file; tick **Play On Awake** and **Loop**.
    3. Press Play (Ctrl+P). Press **Stop** in the section, or stop the game.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** you hear a tone when the game starts, and it loops until stopped.
  - **Known partial:** no log line says which decoder was used.

- [ ] **V-AU-002** Audio Mixer
  - **Map:** 7. **Where:** editor, View menu
  - **Steps:**
    1. Open **View > Panels > Audio Mixer**.
    2. Move the **Master** volume and **Mute** a group (**SFX**, **Music**, **Voice**) while a sound loops in Play.
    3. Try **Duck music under voice**.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** the looping sound gets quieter or silent as you move the sliders; with ducking, music dips when voice plays.
  - **Known partial:** none known.
  - **More:** [audio.md](../../audio.md)

- [ ] **V-AU-003** Spatial sound example
  - **Map:** 7. **Where:** editor, File > Open...
  - **Steps:**
    1. Open `examples/spatial_audio/spatial_audio.okayscene` (the `examples` folder next to the launcher if your release ships it).
    2. Press Play and walk around the sources.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** the sound gets louder and pans left or right as you move. Play starts without a pause, and a second Play starts at once. There is no log line that shows sharing of the decoded clip.
  - **Known partial:** the release may not ship `examples` yet (PR #887).
  - **More:** [../../try_it.md](../../try_it.md)

- [ ] **V-AU-005** Touch controls on screen
  - **Map:** 8, 12. **Where:** editor, GameObject menu
  - **Steps:**
    1. Open `examples/touch_controls/touch_controls.okayscene`, or add **GameObject > UI > Controls > Virtual Joystick** and **Touch Button**.
    2. Press Play and drag the joystick with the mouse.
  - **You should see (expected, not yet seen on Windows):** the on-screen joystick follows the drag; real touch only works on a touch screen.
  - **Known partial:** untested on a touch device.
